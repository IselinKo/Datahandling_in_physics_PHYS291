Root Files: Storing data in a structured and efficient way

We have already used root files to look at histograms and even the gallery
of images from the tutorial.
Here we look in the simplest way at how the files are made and read
plus we also show how to rename the objects and store explanation notes
into the root file.
Quickly run and study these C-files - they illustrate useful methods!

read_histo.C        Reading the histogram written by the standard method Write()
 


Nread_histo.C           Reading the histogram and the explanation text,
