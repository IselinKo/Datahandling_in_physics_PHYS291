WHAT IS THIS?

A root script makes a webpage showing plots-examples

It also shows how to use LaTeX formula - for that one usually needs
the assistence of a webserver - thus the root script via

system(command)

starts a local webserver.

Sources, comments and video are shown.

IT IS RECOMMENDED THAT YOU DOWNLOAD  ROOT_2_WEB.zip to a linux
or macOS machine with root and some of our bash-scripts (present in your bin/ directory)
